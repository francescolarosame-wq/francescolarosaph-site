# Copy DNA Library

A controlled vocabulary, **not** a set of paste-ready paragraphs. Each module
exists so a future page can draw on a consistent idea without repeating a
sentence that already appears elsewhere.

**Rule: no module may be used on more than three pages, and never twice with the
same phrasing.**

## OBSERVATION — currently used on: Luxury Story, Ondrej, Saints, Atarashi, wedding
The core idea: he reads a room rather than directing it.
Verified expressions already live: *"I'd rather miss a shot waiting for the real
one than stage a fake version of it"* (Atarashi) · *"move through the day almost
like a guest rather than a visible production"* (Luxury Story) · *"natural
interactions caught rather than arranged"* (Sicilian Ceremony).
**Warning: closest to overuse. Do not add a fifth variation without cutting one.**

## ADAPTATION — Smashers, Saints, WHITE Milano, ATM
Method changes with the room. Best live example: Smashers — *"Nightlife work is
dark, fast, reactive… A burger launch asks for something more controlled."*
This is the site's most distinctive and least-used idea. **Expand it.**

## LIGHT — Luxury Story, food, Casa Irrera, Aston Martin
Anchored in real technical facts, never abstract: Sicilian summer daylight vs
genuine low light on the same production · bounced not direct flash · flat even
Prague winter light with no artificial fill.

## MOVEMENT — Ondrej, FONDENTE, Saints
Handheld as meaning, not looseness: *"its unstable, mobile quality reflects
emotional turbulence and fragility rather than technical looseness"* (FONDENTE).

## COLOR — Cettina, FONDENTE, videography
Deliberately controlled palettes; DaVinci Resolve where genuinely relevant.
Do not mention grading on pages where it did not drive a decision.

## DOCUMENTARY — weddings cluster
Reportage first, no recreation. **The single most repeated word on the site.**
Use the concept; vary the word.

## PHOTO + VIDEO — wedding.html, Atarashi, Cettina
Two disciplines, one read: *"stills hold a single decisive moment, film carries
the sequence and sound of how the day actually moved."* Strong and currently
used only once. **Reusable.**

## INTERNATIONAL — Ondrej, Cettina, F1, NYBF, WHITE
Prague · Colombo · Barcelona · New York · Boston · Milan. State the place and
the project; never say "worldwide" without one.

## LANGUAGE — Ondrej, wedding, about
Italian / English / Spanish. Best live proof: *"despite not speaking Czech well,
the finished film captured the conversations… accurately enough that the couple
felt everything had been understood."* **The strongest single sentence on the
site.**

## CONTINUITY — Cettina, Smashers, Club Barman, Saints, the wedding family
Clients who came back. **Materially under-used** — see P1-1.

## TECHNICAL CRAFT — per project only
Sony A7 IV · prime lenses · mist filter · bounced flash · multicamera · drone ·
DaVinci Resolve. Never a spec list on a narrative page; only where a choice
changed the result.

## ACCESS — F1, Atarashi, WHITE
FIA accreditation · production pass · a client's own production team. Rare,
concrete, credible. **Under-used.**

## Tone rules
First person. No agency plural. No superlatives without evidence. No "passion",
"journey", "capture memories". Prefer a named venue to an adjective. If a
sentence would survive on any photographer's site, cut it.
