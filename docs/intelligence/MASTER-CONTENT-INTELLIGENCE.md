**Luxury Story (2023) and Sicilian Ceremony (2026) are the same family.**

`luxury-story.html` §06: *"three years later, the same family asked Francesco to
shoot another wedding."*
`sicilian-ceremony.html` §07: *"Three years after filming a wedding for this
family in Sicily, they asked Francesco back for a second one."*

2023 + 3 = 2026. Both pages state it; neither links to the other on that fact,
and the homepage never mentions it. For a wedding buyer this is the strongest
single trust signal on the entire site — a family that hired him, then hired him
again for a second wedding three years later. It is currently buried in §06/§07
of two separate pages.
