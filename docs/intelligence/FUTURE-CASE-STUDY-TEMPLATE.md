# Future Case Study Intake

Run this before writing a single line of a new project page. The point is that
adding a client in future should never restart the research from zero.

## 26 intake questions

**Client & context**
1. Client name, exactly as they write it?
2. Industry / what do they do?
3. Location(s) — every place you actually worked?
4. Date or year? (If you can't date it, say so — we will not invent one.)
5. What was the brief, in their words?
6. Who contacted whom, and through what channel?
7. Why were you chosen — referral, inbound, existing relationship, agency?

**Role & production**
8. Exact role title. Photographer? Videomaker? DP? Camera? Editor?
9. What did *someone else* own? (director, strategy, production decisions)
10. Photo, video, or hybrid — and roughly what split?
11. How many days on site?
12. What was difficult about it?
13. Creative choices you actually made?
14. Technical choices — bodies, lenses, lighting, filters, multicam, drone?

**Output**
15. Final deliverables, itemised?
16. Where was it published?
17. Were your credits visible? Screenshot it.
18. Was it an Instagram collaboration? (Different from a credit.)
19. Did the client leave a Google review?
20. Any editorial or press coverage?
21. Any external link to the site?
22. What outcome can be *stated* — not implied?

**Assets & permissions**
23. Which images exist, and where?
24. Which video exists, and where?
25. Which locations, people and brands can safely be named publicly?
26. What must be marked NEEDS CONFIRMATION?

## Then fill the dossier

Copy the structure from `PROJECT-DATABASE.md` — every field, `NEEDS
CONFIRMATION` where unknown.
