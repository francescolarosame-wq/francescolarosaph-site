## 1. Asset packs — the strongest nine

### 1 · Aston Martin × Peroni — Prague
- **One line** — Three days alone with an Aston Martin DBS Superleggera across Prague, day and night, for a brand partnership campaign.
- **50 words** — Aston Martin approached Francesco directly through social media while he was living in Prague. Over three days he photographed a DBS Superleggera across Old Town Square, Wenceslas Square, Malá Strana, Castle Square and the Powder Tower — working alone, no lighting team, a driver positioning the car, and only the city's own light.
- **100 words** — adds: the campaign context is the Aston Martin / Peroni Libera 0.0% partnership announced February 2021. The constraint that shaped the work was light he did not control: flat overcast winter daylight that behaves like a softbox the size of Prague and suits a black car, then sodium street lighting and floodlit monuments after dark, with wet cobbles acting as a second source. The same car six hours apart is effectively two subjects, which is why the set runs across both. The images were made to support business press and editorial coverage of the partnership.
- **Audience** — automotive editorial, brand and agency creative directors.
- **Hero** — `aston-03` (Old Town Square at night, lit tower, wet cobbles, moon).
- **Supporting** — `aston-11` (Castle Square, St Vitus spires), `aston-07` (Čertovka canal, Malá Strana), `aston-06` (red architectural light, silhouette).
- **URL** — `/aston-martin-peroni.html`
- **Proof** — none at image level. **Do not claim official publication.**
- **Possible destinations** — automotive photography awards and contests; agency/brand portfolio review. **Not Petrolicious** — it is classic/vintage only and a 2019–21 DBS does not qualify.
