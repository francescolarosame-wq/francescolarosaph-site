## 8. Standing rules for all future work

1. A blank field never becomes a guess.
2. "Published by client" ≠ "credited by client". Never merge the two.
3. No location landing pages. Every commercial intent already has a page that earns it.
4. No new case study without a completed [FUTURE-CASE-STUDY-TEMPLATE](FUTURE-CASE-STUDY-TEMPLATE.md).
5. Italian is a parallel edition, never a translation.
6. Do not rewrite copy that is already specific and true. Specificity is the asset here.
