## 1. The proof hierarchy

| Level | Definition | Site label that must be used |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Explicit client credit on published work | "credits the video to him by name" / quote the exact caption |
| **L2** | Instagram collaboration, or a tag naming him | "published … as an Instagram collaboration with Francesco La Rosa" / "tagging Francesco La Rosa" |
| **L3** | Client published content that is clearly his, without a written credit | "published by @client — slides 1, 2, 8… are his" |
| **L4** | Google review | attributed by name, role and company, with a link to Google |
| **L5** | Editorial publication | publication, author, date, link |
| **L6** | External backlink / press mention / third-party citation | named and linked |
| **L7** | First-party case-study statement | Francesco's own voice — always allowed, never dressed as external proof |

**The two confusions that must never happen:**
- "published by client" is **not** "credited by client".
- "Instagram collaboration" is **not** "written credit".

The site currently observes both distinctions correctly on all eight published proof blocks.

## 4. What may be claimed

✅ **Yes:**
- "Cettina Bucca credits the work by name on its own account."
- "Saints Music Atelier credits the photography in three published captions."
- "Atarashi published his photographs."
- "New York Business Fellowship published the work as an Instagram collaboration."
- Quote the Google review in full, attributed, linked.
- Once implemented: "Featured in Gazzetta del Sud, February 2021."

❌ **No:**
- Any credit claim for **WHITE Milano**, **Smashers** or **Sicilian Ceremony** — an
  authenticated review found 0 credits across 7, 4 and 0 posts respectively.
- Any award, festival selection or metric that is not on the record. FONDENTE's "submitted to
  five festivals in Rome" and "140K views" are the limit of what is verified.
- Any client outcome number (reach, sales, followers). None has ever been verified.
- `AggregateRating` schema on one review.
