# EN CONTENT AUDIT
English is the principal editorial benchmark. Every page below was read in full on 4 Sep 2026.

**Verdict scale:** KEEP · MINOR EDIT · REWRITE · MISSING · VERIFY · REMOVE
Word counts are body prose (`<p>` text), which is what a reader and a language model actually
consume — not raw HTML weight.

---

## 1. Headline finding

**The English copy is genuinely good, and most of it should not be touched.**

It is specific where most photographer sites are generic. It names venues, dates, cameras,
lens choices, guest ratios, the number of hours on site, why a background was chosen before an
angle, and — unusually — it says when a relationship ended and why. That specificity is the
site's competitive asset. The instruction "do not overwrite good existing copy" applies to
most of this site.

Three problems are real, and only three:

1. **One factual self-contradiction** (D-01, Luxury Story).
2. **A split narrative voice** — the site is "I" on five case studies and "Francesco" on twelve.
3. **Two hub pages are far thinner than the case studies they are supposed to lead into.**

## 3. Cross-cutting findings

### D-06 · Narrative voice (P1)

| Voice | Pages |
|---|---|
| **First person ("I")** | atarashi, saints, club-barman, dose, atm-messina, wedding, contact, about, index |
| **Third person ("Francesco")** | cettina-bucca, white-milano, luxury-story, sicilian-ceremony, ondrej-dominika, smashers, new-york-business-fellowship, aston-martin-peroni, formula-1-barcelona, casa-irrera, fondente |

A reader moving from `saints.html` ("I shoot loose and close") to `luxury-story.html`
("Francesco was expected to move through the day") meets two different narrators describing
one person. Third-person self-description also reads as agency copy, which contradicts the
positioning in §3 of the master file.

**Recommendation:** standardise on **first person** for case studies. It matches the About
page, the homepage, the FAQ and the strongest case studies (Atarashi, Saints), and it is
truthful to a one-person practice. Keep third person only where the sentence is genuinely
about someone else's decision ("Aston Martin contacted Francesco directly").

This is a mechanical, low-risk pass on 11 pages. It should be the *first* thing the next
copy step does, because every later edit inherits the voice.

### Proof density

| Page | Proof carried |

| new-york-business-fellowship | 2 collaborations |

Thirteen case studies rest entirely on Francesco's own voice. That is legitimate — Level 7 in
the proof hierarchy is a valid tier — but it means the site's credibility currently sits on
four pages. See [PROOF-AUTHORITY-MAP.md](PROOF-AUTHORITY-MAP.md).

### What must not change

- The pricing transparency on `services.html`.
- The role honesty on `atm-messina.html` and `fondente`.
- The refusal to claim a WHITE Milano credit.
- The Google review's full five paragraphs, in both languages.
- The alt-text quality (median 15 words, zero "— 3" placeholders).
- The specificity: cameras, hours, guest ratios, venue names, why a relationship ended.
