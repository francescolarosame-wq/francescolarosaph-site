## The headline number

**Referring domains: 0. Real backlinks: 0.**

Both of the sources most likely to be links turned out not to be:

- **Gazzetta del Sud** is a genuine, high-quality editorial feature — but it
  predates the website by five years and contains no link to it.
- **Cherrydeck** has the URL in the bio, but Cherrydeck renders bio text without
  auto-linking, so it is not crawlable.

This corrects an assumption carried into STEP 27: Cherrydeck is **not** currently
a backlink.

## The two cheapest real wins

1. **Cherrydeck** — move the URL out of the free-text bio into the profile's
   dedicated *Website* field, which renders as a real anchor. Likely a 2-minute
   change that converts an unlinked mention into an actual backlink.
2. **Gazzetta del Sud** — ask them to add a link to the 2021 feature, or, more
   realistically, pitch a follow-up. The 2021 piece already establishes the
   entity; a 2026 follow-up (Atarashi, F1, Colombo) would be a genuine
   `EDITORIAL BACKLINK`.
