# COPY DNA LIBRARY
A controlled vocabulary and a bank of editorial modules.

**The rule that governs this whole file: these are ingredients, not a paragraph to paste.**
A module that appears on every page stops being a differentiator and becomes filler. Each
module below carries a *usage limit* — how many pages may use it, and which.

---

## 1. Voice

| Dimension | Rule |
|---|---|
| Person | **First person singular.** "I", never "we", never "Francesco" in his own descriptions (D-06) |
| Register | Plain, concrete, unhurried. A working professional explaining a decision, not a brand talking about itself |
| Tense | Past for what happened, present for how he works |
| Adjectives | Earn them. "Cinematic" once per page at most; never as a substitute for a description |
| Numbers | Always preferred over adjectives — "about two and a half hours", "roughly 80% of the guests", "three nights, three venues" |
| Admissions | Keep them. "The relationship did not continue" and "most of the real problem-solving was logistics, not anything creative" are the two most credible sentences on the site |

### Banned constructions

- "Passionate about capturing moments"
- "Turning moments into memories"
- "Your special day"
- "State-of-the-art equipment"
- "We" / "our team" / "the studio"
- "Award-winning" (no award exists)
- "Luxury" as a self-description — the work shows it or it does not
- Any sentence that would be equally true of any other photographer

## 2. Vocabulary bank

| Theme | Words that belong | Words to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Method | observe, wait, read, adapt, minimal intervention, unposed, documentary, reportage | capture magic, curate emotions |
| Light | flat, even, directional, backlit, bounced, golden, low light, silhouette | breathtaking, stunning |
| Movement | handheld, stabilised, loose, close, gimbal, drone, multicam | dynamic, epic |
| Colour | grade, palette, calibrated contrast, mist filter, DaVinci Resolve | vibrant, cinematic colours |
| Access | production pass, FIA accreditation, embedded, inside the production | exclusive access |
| Relationship | ongoing, returning, since 2021, three years, came back | trusted by, partnered with |

## 3. The nine modules

Each has a fixed usage limit. Exceeding it is how a site starts to sound generic.

---

### M1 · OBSERVATION
*Working without forcing a moment.*
**Limit: 3 pages.** Currently `about`, `wedding`, `atarashi`. Correct — do not add a fourth.

> "Most of what's here isn't posed — it's timing. I'd rather miss a shot waiting for the real
> one than stage a fake version of it."

---

### M2 · ADAPTATION
*Different rooms need different methods.*
**Limit: 4 pages, only where genuinely different rooms exist.** Currently `atarashi` (three
venues), `smashers` (nightlife → counter), `white-milano` (a designer interview one hour,
evening coverage the next), `wedding` (tent vs three-day destination).

---

### M3 · LIGHT
*Light as the subject, not a setting.*
**Limit: use only where a specific light problem was solved.** Currently `luxury-story` (full
sun then genuine darkness), `food` (hard flash in dark rooms), `aston-martin` (flat Prague
winter light, no added lighting), `casa-irrera` (bounced flash across a dozen desserts).
Never as a generic statement.

---

### M4 · DOCUMENTARY
*Why unposed matters commercially, not just aesthetically.*
**Limit: the wedding cluster + `about`.** Elsewhere it is assumed, not restated.

---

### M5 · PHOTO + VIDEO
*Why the two practices reinforce each other.*
**Limit: 3 — `videography`, `wedding`, `photography`.** The current formulation is the best
version and should not be rewritten:

> "Stills hold a single decisive moment, film carries the sequence and sound of how the day
> actually moved — on a wedding that books both, I'm reading the same room for two different
> outcomes at once, not shooting one and treating the other as a byproduct."

---

### M6 · CONTINUITY
*Clients who come back.* **This is the most under-used module on the site.*
**Should appear on 5 pages; currently reads strongly on 3.**

| Page | The proof |
|---|---|
| `cettina-bucca` | three years, continuous |
| `sicilian-ceremony` / `luxury-story` | the same family, three years apart |
| `smashers` | the founder since 2021, across two genres |
| `saints` | *"a collaboration that continues over time"* — the client's own words |
| `white-milano` | multiple recurring editions |
| `dose` | a run of nights, one season |

Continuity is the strongest commercial argument on this site and it is currently scattered
rather than stated. **This is the P1 editorial opportunity.**

---

### M7 · INTERNATIONAL
*Working outside home.*
**Limit: 4.** Currently `about`, `ondrej-dominika`, `cettina-bucca` (Colombo),
`new-york-business-fellowship`. Do **not** turn it into a "worldwide" boast on every page —
the world map on the homepage already does that job.

---

### M8 · LANGUAGE
*Italian, English, Spanish.*
**Limit: 3 — `about`, `wedding`, `contact`.** It is only commercially meaningful where a
briefing actually happens across languages. The Ondrej & Dominika page makes the stronger,
inverted version of this argument — that the film worked in a language he does *not* speak —
and that is the single best line on the site for international couples.

---

### M9 · TECHNICAL CRAFT
*Bodies, lenses, stabilisation, grade.*
**Limit: only where the choice shaped the outcome.** Currently `casa-irrera` (A7 IV, bounced
flash), `luxury-story` (primes across two light conditions), `ondrej-dominika` (mist filter,
natural light only), `fondente` (handheld as emotional register), `rental` (the full list, by
design). Never a spec list on a case study.

## 4. Differentiation matrix — no two pages may prove the same thing

The failure mode for a portfolio site is five wedding pages that all say "documentary and
cinematic". Each page is assigned **one** thing to prove.

### Weddings

| Page | Proves, and only this |
|---|---|
| `luxury-story` | **Discretion at scale** — three days, a private family, ~80% international guests, a film that was never content |
| `sicilian-ceremony` | **Return** — the same family came back three years later |
| `ondrej-dominika` | **Cross-cultural understanding** — a day read accurately in a language he does not speak |
| Josh & Julie | Weddings outside Sicily, photography |
| Noah & Jessie | Small, fast, intimate — two hours, mostly candid |
| `wedding.html` (hub) | The two disciplines, and how the method flexes by scale |

### Fashion

| Page | Proves |
|---|---|
| `cettina-bucca` | **Continuity** — three years as a brand's ongoing visual voice, plus international escalation |
| `white-milano` | **Working inside someone else's production team**, across editions |

## 5. Reusable CTA patterns

Currently in use and working — keep the structure, vary the noun:

> "For couples planning a destination wedding who want a film built around discretion, not spectacle."
> "For event producers and brands who need coverage from inside the production — not just from the crowd."
> "For public bodies and companies that need service, events and campaigns documented clearly."

Formula: **"For [specific audience] who need [specific outcome] — not [the generic version]."**
It works because it disqualifies as much as it invites. Do not replace it with "Get in touch".
