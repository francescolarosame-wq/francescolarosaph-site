Before you write

What happens after I send an enquiry?

You tell me your date, location and what you're planning. I reply with availability and a written quote, usually within a couple of days. From there we agree on locations, timing and the look you want before anything is booked.

Do you travel outside Sicily?

Yes. I'm based in Messina and available worldwide. Travel within 50km of Messina is included; 50–150km is quoted at €0.50/km plus an overnight stay if needed; anything outside Sicily, including destination weddings, is quoted case by case.

Do you shoot both wedding films and wedding photography?

Yes, as two complementary services — documentary wedding films and documentary wedding photography. Some couples book both, some book one. See examples on the Weddings page.

Is your wedding coverage documentary, or do you direct and stage moments?

Documentary first, with minimal intervention. The approach is to be present without being intrusive — following what's actually happening rather than arranging it. Staged, posed sequences are the exception, not the default.

How long until I receive my photos or film?

Photos are typically delivered in 5–10 working days, video in 10–20 working days. An express option (48–72 hours, +40%) is available when timing is tight.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Weddings, brand and commercial work, hospitality and venues, motorsport, and events and celebrations. If your project doesn't fit neatly into one of those, ask — the form has a category for that too.

What can I do with the images or footage I receive?

Personal and social use is included with every booking. Commercial use is included for 1 year within Italy. Unlimited use or a full buyout, and exclusivity, are quoted case by case — ask if your project needs either.

What happens to the information I submit in this form?

It's used to prepare your quote and, if the project goes ahead, to deliver it — nothing more. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
