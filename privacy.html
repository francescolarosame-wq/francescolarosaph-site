How your enquiry
data is handled.

This page explains what happens to the information you submit through the contact form on this website. Last updated 17 August 2026.

01

Who is responsible for your data

Francesco La Rosa (P.IVA 03765610831), based in Messina, Italy, is the controller for personal data submitted through the contact form on francescolarosaph.com. You can reach the controller via the contact page.

02

03

The lead-fit label

When you submit the form, a small script running in your own browser (not on a server, and not using AI) labels the enquiry HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW FIT. The label is calculated only from the project type, budget range and timeline you entered in that same submission — no other data source is used.

This calculation is used only to help Francesco prioritize the order in which he replies to enquiries. It does not decide whether you receive a reply, does not filter or block any submission, and does not produce any automated acceptance, rejection, or other legal or similarly significant decision about you. A human reads and responds to every enquiry regardless of its label. The label is sent along with your message and is not stored or used for any other purpose.

04

Purpose and legal basis

Your data is processed to respond to your enquiry and, if a project goes ahead, to prepare and deliver it. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — processing necessary to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract, or to perform a contract already agreed. No marketing use, profiling with legal effect, or automated decision-making occurs.

06

Analytics

This site is prepared for Google Analytics 4, but GA4 is not active — no analytics data is currently being collected or sent anywhere. The code that would activate it only runs on the live production domain, never on a development copy of the site, and it will not go live until a real GA4 property exists. This policy will be updated with GA4's specific data practices (cookies, retention, IP handling) before that happens — not after.

07

Retention

Francesco retains enquiry emails for as long as reasonably needed to respond, and — if a project is booked — for the duration of the professional/commercial relationship and any legal retention period that applies afterward (e.g. Italian tax and accounting record-keeping requirements). Formspree's own retention of submission data is governed by its own policy (see Section 5) and is outside this site's direct control.

08

Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, and you can object to or ask to restrict its processing. To exercise any of these, contact Francesco via the contact page. If you believe your data has been mishandled, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

09

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or its tools change — most notably when GA4 is activated. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.
