The program

New York Business Fellowship, founded by Giovanni Gallina and Carlos Tacchetto, runs a selective one-week NYC business immersion for students, recent graduates, young professionals and founders — mentor sessions, company visits, networking and case competitions connecting participants with the New York business ecosystem. NYBF describes an acceptance rate under 15%, cohorts drawn from four continents, and 10+ mentor sessions a week.

02

Pre-launch

Contact came through a local/founder connection. Ahead of the program itself, Francesco produced pre-launch video — content built to introduce the cohort to New York and build anticipation before the week began.
