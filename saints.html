The event

Saints runs nightlife and music events around Messina, including at Ikesia Beach Club — the kind of night built on a DJ set, a crowd that shows up to move, and an atmosphere that has to be felt rather than described.

02

My assignment

Photography and multicamera coverage through the night, then cutting it all into a single recap video — the piece that has to carry the whole event's energy for people who weren't there.

03

How I approached it

Multicam nightlife coverage means being in more than one place in the same set — the DJ booth, the crowd, the door, the moment someone's dancing exactly the way the night is supposed to feel. Low light, constant movement, no do-overs. I shoot loose and close, then find the shape of the night in the edit rather than trying to plan it shot-by-shot beforehand — the recap only works if it feels like being there, not like a highlight reel assembled after the fact.
